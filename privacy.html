Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Pick'em Poker is a free poker game on the web at pickem-poker.com and on iOS. You can play without an account. This page explains what we collect if you do sign in, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address, a unique account ID, and your name if you choose to share it. Sign in with Apple lets you hide your real email address.
  • Game history. For signed-in players we store the hands you play and their results, so we can show your history and stats.
  • Profile and leaderboard. Your display name, and a leaderboard name if you opt in to the public leaderboard.
  • Settings on your device. Preferences like dark mode and four-color deck are saved on your device only and are never sent to us.

We don't use advertising, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your game history and stats attached to your account.
  • To show your leaderboard name, win rate, and number of hands on the public leaderboard, only if you opt in.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in.
  • Google and Apple verify your identity when you use their sign-in.
  • Vercel hosts the website.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data until you delete it. You can clear your hand history, or permanently delete your account and everything attached to it, at any time: open the menu, choose your profile, and tap delete account. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone.

Children

Pick'em Poker is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
